Apollo Quant

Built to protect capital first, grow it second.

Apollo Quant is an AI-assisted, systematic trading strategy designed around risk discipline, aiming to outperform the S&P 500 with meaningfully less drawdown and a much shorter time spent recovering losses, validated across over two decades of historical backtesting before a single live trade.

What we do

Apollo Quant designs and operates AI-assisted trading systems that combine systematic, rules-based decision-making with machine-learning techniques to manage risk and position sizing. Our approach favors consistency and capital preservation over chasing short-term outperformance. We'd rather compound steadily through many market regimes than swing for one great year.

The strategy trades across multiple, largely uncorrelated asset classes rather than concentrating in one market, which means it can also serve as a diversifying allocation alongside a traditional equity-heavy portfolio, not just a standalone bet.

Why systematic, why AI

Discretionary trading is vulnerable to the trader's own psychology. Fear, overconfidence, and fatigue all creep into decisions made under pressure. Apollo Quant removes that variable: every position is sized by explicit, pre-defined risk rules, and machine-learning models are used specifically to adapt that risk management to changing market conditions, not to chase predictions. The result is a process that behaves the same way on its best day and its worst day.

How it works

01

Our models continuously read market data and generate a systematic view of where risk and opportunity sit across the assets we trade.

02

Position sizing and exposure are set algorithmically, with explicit risk limits. No single signal or event can move the portfolio outside its risk budget.

03

Trades execute automatically, around the clock, with no manual intervention or emotional decision-making in the loop.

Who it's for

Apollo Quant is built for investors who'd rather compound steadily than chase the best possible year. People who value a shorter, shallower drawdown over a higher ceiling, and who want a systematic process they can understand the logic of, even without seeing every rule. If your priority is capital preservation with real, participatory upside, this is built for you. If you're looking to maximize short-term returns regardless of volatility, it probably isn't.

Where we're headed

We're currently validating our strategies with our own capital. Our next step is making this available to others through a copy-trading offering, so investors can access systematic, risk-managed returns without having to build or run the system themselves.
